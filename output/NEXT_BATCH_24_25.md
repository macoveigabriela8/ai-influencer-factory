## Why these two, and why NOT the 957k

Source play count has been a **bad predictor** of our own results:

| our reel | source plays | our views |
|---|---|---|
| Glass of water | 4,047,769 | ~1,400 |
| Dreams of the dead | 1,384,721 | ~2,256 |
| **The first daughter** | **580,071** | **21,112** |

The vein predicts, the source size does not. `DVEI18AgH7h` (957k) is morning
declarations — the same ground as reels 12 and 20, both of which underperformed.
Taking it would re-test a failed vein. Both reels here sit in the emotional /
inner-life vein that produced our only hit, so the batch is a clean experiment:
**both clear 5,000 views → hypothesis holds. Both land near 700 → it is dead.**

`DVEI18AgH7h` stays on the shortlist for later.

## What the frame grids showed (three sources, one pattern)

| | 957k (standing) | 441k (table) | 228k (floor) |
|---|---|---|---|
| pose | standing, full body | **seated, table, legs visible** | **full body, cross-legged on floor** |
| setting | bridge, temple roof | interior hall, red columns | ornate hall, gold Buddha, lanterns |
| foreground | none | **open book + lit candle** | none |
| captions | white text, drop shadow, no box | same | same |
| CTA card | **none** | **none** | **none** |
| close | "comment peace and I send you something powerful" | "The 30 day healing journey is there for that process" | — |

**Constant across all three: interior or ornate warm setting, body visible (not
chest-up), no CTA card, small unboxed captions, and a spoken close that names
the product.**

Ours (reel 7) differs on: chest-up framing, outdoor overcast, no light source in
frame, boxed captions, and a persistent CTA card.

⚠️ **Do NOT copy the standing pose.** PT6 proved a standing anchor makes kling
sway forward and back over a long take. Seated only — which is also what the
441k and 228k sources do.

⚠️ **Subtitle gate:** a full-body shot puts floor and robe texture in the
y1150–1650 band and fails EVERY frame at uniform density. That is scene texture,
not burned subtitles. Eyeball frames before re-rolling anything in this batch.

## Answers to the two standing questions

**Do they have a keyword CTA?** Yes — **spoken, one word, in both**, exactly like
the sources. Measured across 62 of his transcripts: comment CTA in **61/62
(98%)**, "the link is in my profile" in **62/62 (100%)**. Both scripts carry

single word ("peace", "amen", "rest"). Our existing multi-word keywords like
"my paths are opening" are why several automations have never fired.
