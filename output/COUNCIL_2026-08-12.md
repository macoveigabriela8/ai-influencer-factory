## Verdicts by seat

**Monetization — "we are not underpriced, we are under-packaged."**
monkchen.com sells the same 30-day PDF shape at $29.99->$14.95 with three
bonuses, scarcity and a guarantee; we sell a bare $14.95 file. A $47 anchored
bundle using the audiobook we already built (`output/book/`) roughly doubles
revenue per buyer with zero extra traffic. Explicit DON'T: no physical product
(mingsanhealing's supplement rides 397k followers and real inventory).

**Content — content is NOT the binding constraint.**
At 843 median vs the 134 size-matched benchmark, per-post distribution is
already ~6x normal. The problem is topic selection: only 1 of 10 published reels
sat in the vein that works. **Reel 7 "first daughter" = 31,356 views; no other
published reel cleared 2,700.** Of 16 scheduled EN reels, 13 were ritual/habit.
- **Source play count is ANTI-correlated with our results** — reel 13 (source
  718,914) -> 222 views; the 415k-source reel -> 199; a 75,500-source reel ->
  2,147. Delete play count as a selection input; rank by VEIN and by
  cross-account proof.
- **Require a second-person instruction in the first sentence.** Reel 13 is the
  only published reel without one and is the only Kaion video on either YouTube
  channel with zero comments ever.
- Best unused topics, both cross-proven: `DVOYptXAOH3` (430,140 plays, **3,927
  comments = 0.91%**) and `DT5MoDUAL6D` (461,853, identity vein).
- Cadence: n=10 cannot separate 2/day from 1/day. Don't cut on this evidence.

**Distribution — YES, this is the binding constraint.**
Same file, different platform: **13.2 views per subscriber on YouTube vs 2.5 per
follower on Instagram.** Socialinsider (69M videos, Aug 2026) by follower
bracket, under 5K: **Shorts 15,160 · Reels 625 · TikTok 350.** TikTok only
overtakes Instagram at 10-50K followers; its higher engagement rate is
per-follower and inflates trivially at low counts.
⚠️ Its headline recommendation ("push all 35 reels to YouTube") was **checked and
found wrong** — YouTube was already scheduled 2/day through Aug 18. The real gap
was 5 reels, of which 15 and 17 are defective (burned subtitles, re-verified)
and 24/25 have now been scheduled.

**Skeptic — the reach gates do not measure money.**
At 0.015% click-through a 250k-view outlier yields ~37 page views ≈ 0-1 sales,
so **the Oct 7 "outlier >250k" gate can be passed while earning $0.** Also:
the source account is in decline — `analysis/VIRALITY_ANALYSIS.md` measures his
median falling 27,161 -> 3,490 over five months, **r(date, log plays) = -0.559,
n=266**, an order of magnitude stronger than any script feature. We are copying
a decaying format one cold-start window later into ~25 competing clones.

## Beliefs this review invalidated

Each of these was being used to steer decisions and does not survive checking:

1. **"Hashtags cut views 31.7% (Metricool)"** — could not be verified. Our own
   data leans the other way: hashtag-only captions median ~810 vs three
   text-caption posts at median 1,477.
2. **"Rank sources by play count"** — anti-correlated in our own numbers.
3. **"TikTok accounts need a 10-14 day warm-up or they get banned"** — traces
   entirely to anti-detect-browser vendors. TikTok states follower count and
   past performance are NOT ranking factors. TikTok penalises **automation**,
   not newness — so manual-only is right, the ban rationale was not.
4. **"TikTok is where our reach is"** — at under 5K followers it is the WEAKEST
   of the three, ~half of Reels. It is a bet on eventual outliers, not on
   near-term reach.
5. **"30-60s is the target length"** — `sop/SOP.md:63` measures his catalogue at
   143s median with r = +0.113 (longer slightly better). Line 70 states the
   30-60s choice was **economic, for Seedance at $0.205/s**. Kling is $0.0383/s,
   so a 150s reel now costs ~$5.75 and the entire basis has evaporated. The SOP
   prescribed shipping a 150s control; it was never shipped. **Still unrun.**

Also flagged as unsourced and not to be quoted: Threads "video 5.55% ER",
"collab posts get 2.2x reach", "Pinterest drives 33% more referral traffic",
Snapchat "$0.10-0.30 per 1,000 views", "new TikTok accounts get a boost".

## Constraints discovered

- **Instagram links are not clickable in captions or comments** (verified 2026).
  Only the bio link (up to 5) and Story link stickers are one-tap. A comment
  keyword -> DM delivers a tappable button. That is the entire IG money path.
- **YouTube YPP bars monetization for AI personas on health/wellness topics**
  ("an AI 'doctor' providing... wellness remedies"). Does not affect the Gumroad
  funnel; blocks ever monetising the channel itself.
- **A third-party watermark makes a video ineligible for TikTok's For You feed**
  (first-party). Our masters carry none — keep it that way.
- **Pinterest** is the only unused surface that carries an outbound link and
  takes our 9:16 file unmodified — but it auto-labels AI content and lets users
  choose "see fewer AI-modified Pins".
