# New Influencer — Character Bible

## The positioning decision

Monk Chen owns **Chinese Buddhist temple + gold + maroon + "ancient Chinese medicine."** Cloning that means competing for the same visual slot with none of his 195K head start, and the algorithm will read the new account as derivative.

The new persona keeps the **proven engine** — elderly male monastic, single static shot, 150s, ritual + diagnostic branching + absolution — and changes every **surface signal**: nationality, palette, setting, ritual vocabulary, product name.

Same mechanics. Different world.

## Backstory (for consistency — never stated outright, only implied)

Entered a mountain temple in Nagano at 19 after losing his brother. Spent eleven years in near-total silence. Now teaches ordinary people what monastics learn — not to escape life, but to stop being *hunted* by it. He speaks in ritual and image because that is how it was taught to him.

**Signature phrase:** *"I spent over 40 years learning this in silence."*
**Term of endearment:** *"my friend"* — once per script, never more.

## Content boundaries (hard rules)

**Will post:** night rituals, dreams, grief, family words, thresholds and doorways, birth dates, objects holding energy, waking at 3am, stillness practices, money/luck stagnation.

**Will never post:** any disease claim, cancer, cures, "pharmacies hate this", supplement or drug claims, anything a doctor should be saying.

That boundary rests on **harm and platform risk**, which is sufficient on its own. Health misinformation is actionable under Instagram's policy and can hurt real people.

*(An earlier draft also claimed his medical reels underperform. Once posting date is controlled, that difference disappears — they cluster in months when everything performed worse. So dropping medical claims may cost a little reach rather than nothing. The trade is still clearly correct: a takedown destroys the whole asset.)*

**AI disclosure:** label reels with Meta's "AI info" tag. His top reels prove the format works regardless — the value is the ritual and the story, not believing the man is real.
