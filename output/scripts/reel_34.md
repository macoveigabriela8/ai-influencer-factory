## Script — spoken, verbatim (196 words, ~88s at our measured 133 wpm)

> If you still see your mother's name light up your phone, that is not ordinary.
> That is a blessing.
>
> If your father still calls you, even for no reason at all, that is a blessing.
>
> If your brother or your sister sends you nonsense, jokes, small things that do
> not matter, that is life still reaching for you.
>
> If your grandparents still remember your name, your face, your voice — that is
> a quiet miracle.
>
> If someone says, call me when you get home, they are telling you that your life
> matters to them.
>
> If someone prays for you when you are not in the room, that is protection you
> will never see.
>
> Do not rush past these moments. Do not let them become normal.
>
> Because one day the phone will be quiet. The messages will stop. The names will
> not appear again. And only then will you understand that these were never small
> things. They were the riches of a life.
>
> Comment blessing, and I will send you what to do tonight.
>
> Noticing what you still have is where this begins. Holding on to it when the
> world is loud is the harder part. Everything I know is inside The Quiet Between
> Thoughts. The link is in my profile.
