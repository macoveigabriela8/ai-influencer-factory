## Script — spoken, verbatim (188 words, ~85s at our measured 133 wpm)

> Your personality creates your personal reality. Most people never realize this.
>
> The life you are living right now — the people around you, the experiences you
> attract — is a reflection of one thing. How you think, how you act, how you
> feel. That is your personality.
>
> So begin to notice your inner world. Become aware of the thoughts that repeat
> when no one is watching. Watch how you speak, how you react, how you carry
> yourself. Notice which emotion you live in every day.

> that is who they are. It is not who you are. It is only who you have been
> practicing to be.
>
> When you start observing yourself, you rise above your old patterns. A new
> thought leads to a new choice. A new choice becomes a new action. A new action
> brings a new experience. And the body begins to feel what the mind has
> learned. That is how change becomes real.
>
> Comment practice, and I will send you the first one.
>
> Noticing the pattern is where this begins. It is not where it ends — the body
> needs far longer than a moment to believe you. Everything I know is inside The
