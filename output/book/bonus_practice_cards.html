How to use these

One page. One act. One night.

These are the practices I have given away one at a time, to the people who asked for them. Each one is a single act, done with the body, at a stated time. Not an idea to agree with. An act.

Do not read them all tonight. Open one page, do what it says, and close the book. A practice you performed once is worth more than thirty you understood.

The thirty days in the main book build on these. These stand alone if you have only tonight.

THE PRACTICE CARDS

Practice 01

Home

My friend, here is the full house blessing.

Open every window and let the old air leave. Walk through each room in silence, touch the walls, and say: from today, this space is a place of peace. Throw a handful of salt into the toilet and flush what is not yours. Then light one white candle in the center of your home and speak what you wish to live there: harmony, peace, rest. It is not the ritual that changes the house. It is the intention that changes you.

Practice 02

Step

My friend, here is the first practice.

Tomorrow, before your mind can argue, put on your shoes and stand outside your door for two minutes. Do not go anywhere. Feel the air, the light, the sound. That is all. The day after, walk to the corner and back. You do not have to feel better to go out. You go out to feel better.

Practice 03

Water

My friend, here is the ritual.

Tonight, fill a clear glass with water and place it beside your bed. As you set it down, say quietly: take from me what is not mine. Sleep. In the morning, do not drink it. Look at it once, then pour it away and say: it has done its work. Do this for three nights. The rest of what I know is inside The Quiet Between Thoughts. When you are ready, reply YES and I will leave you a small gift.

Good, my friend.

Enough

Good, my friend.

Practice 10

Peace

When it returns, my friend, do this.

Breathe in for four counts, out for six. Ten breaths. The longer exhale tells the body the danger has passed, and the mind follows the body.
