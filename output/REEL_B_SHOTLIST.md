# Reel B — Flow shot list

**22 clips · 158.5s · 330 credits** (15 credits per 10s generation)

Flow caps a generation at 10s, so the reel is built as a chain: generate clip 01,
press `+` on the timeline, generate 02 as an extension of it, and so on. Extending
carries lighting and pose forward — a fresh generation resets both, which is where
drift across a long reel comes from.

Every clip ends on a sentence. A clip boundary mid-sentence is the one place a
generation seam becomes audible.

Clip length is capped at 25 words: 10s at his measured 161.6 wpm is 26.9 words, and
the headroom absorbs the pause Flow adds at a sentence end. Because credits are
charged per generation rather than per second, an under-filled clip costs the same
as a full one — so these are packed to the cap, with the slack spread evenly rather
than left as a stub at the end.

Paste each dialogue into the prompt box as:  `He says: "..."`
