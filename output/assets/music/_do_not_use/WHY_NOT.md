# Do not use these tracks

`anguish.mp3` and `ossuary1.mp3` are **Kevin MacLeod** (ISRC USUAN1400047,
album tag "Royalty Free"). His catalogue is licensed **CC BY**, which requires
**visible attribution wherever the work appears**.

That is incompatible with how this project publishes: captions are hashtags
only, exactly like the source accounts, so there is nowhere to put the credit.
Using either track without a credit line would be a licence breach on every
platform we post to.

They are kept rather than deleted only so nobody re-downloads them thinking
they are a fresh find. Do not pass them to `MUSIC=`.
