# @master_kaion — growth & conversion strategy (2026-08-07)

## The honest frame first

The target given is 1M followers in 30 days at 1% purchase conversion. Written
plainly: that outcome exists, but it is not a plan — it is a lottery prize.
Even the account we copy did not do it: @monkcheen's own median video is
**8,651 plays**, 86% of his 271 posts sit under 30k, and his entire account is
built on ONE day-one 4M-play distribution break plus a second at day five.
His engine is: post daily forever, and be positioned so that when the algorithm
hands you a rocket, the profile converts it.

So the strategy is not "make a viral video" — nobody can schedule that. It is:
**maximize the number of lottery tickets per dollar, and make sure that on the
day one hits, every layer of the funnel underneath it is already working.**
That second part is fully controllable, and right now it is broken in three
places (§2).

What a good 30 days actually looks like: 45–60 posts, several in the
50k–500k band, follower base in the low tens of thousands, and a working
product funnel measured in real purchases. That compounds. A 1M vanity target
does not.

## 1. The math that matters

Revenue = views × profile-tap rate × follow/link-tap rate × purchase rate.

We currently optimize ONLY views (scripts). The three downstream rates are at
or near zero not because of content quality but because of missing plumbing:

| leak | state | fix cost |
|---|---|---|
| Grid looks like one video ×5 (variety 7.3 vs his 59.1) | FIXED — 6 anchors generated 2026-08-07 | $0.12, done |
| "The link is in my profile" → **no link exists** | OPEN — every reel breaks its own promise | $0 + product page |
| No comment funnel — his DM/comment mechanic is half the sell | OPEN | $0, manual minutes/day |

**Nothing else gets budget until the link exists.** 62/62 of his videos close
on that line; ours copy it; the profile has nothing behind it.

## 2. Funnel plumbing (week 1, mostly free)

1. **Product page live** — "The Quiet Between Thoughts" on Gumroad/LemonSqueezy
   at $9–17. A 30-page PDF + audio is enough for v1; improve after first sales.
   Until it exists we are running ads for an empty shop.
2. **Bio**: keep 2 lines + link. Line under the name should promise the
   product's outcome, not describe the account.
3. **Comment engine** (his real conversion trick): every reel instructs a
   comment word ("Peace", "Abundance"). Reply to EVERY commenter within the
   first hour — replies double as algorithmic engagement signals and as the
   DM-adjacent touch that warms buyers. 15 min/day, user-side.
4. **AI-info toggle** on every post (policy safety = account survival).
5. Follow 50–100 adjacent spiritual accounts (3 following reads bot-like).

## 3. Content engine (the ticket printer)

- **Cadence**: hold 2 reels/day + 1 story/day. His whole catalogue = daily
  posting since March. Volume IS the strategy; 45–60 tickets/month.
- **Cost per ticket**: 60s reels at ~$4.49 (kling $0.0748/s) — not 90s at
  $6.70. Same hook power, 33% more tickets per dollar, fewer joins = less
  face drift.
- **Source selection**: his catalogue first (proven hooks, verbatim), ranked by
  comment-rate, truth-rule filtered (SOP Part 0b). ~15 clean sources remain.
- **Format spread per week** (stop monoculture): 8 ritual reels, 2–3
  affirmation/comment-bait (his best unused comment rate is an affirmation
  video at 1.665%), 1–2 emotional-family vein. Track which band OUR audience
  responds to; double down after 2 weeks of OUR data, not his.
- **Every reel**: hook verbatim, drawn comment CTA ≠ closing CTA, spoken pivot
  + "everything I know is inside…" + link line — the funnel depends on it.

## 4. Multiply distribution for free

The same file posts to **TikTok + YouTube Shorts** at zero marginal production
cost. Both platforms have API AI-disclosure flags (set them), both are where
faceless/AI spiritual content currently over-performs, and both give a
brand-new account a fairer cold start than IG gives one. This is the single
cheapest way to triple ticket count. Blotato supports both — needs the user to
connect the accounts.

## 5. Study the winners properly (needs Apify top-up — currently 402)

One scrape, ~$5–10 of Apify credit, two jobs:
1. **5–10 fast-growing monk/spiritual-AI accounts** — pull their top 20 reels
   each, transcribe, extract hook patterns we can't see in monkcheen alone
   (what works for accounts WITHOUT a 4M lucky break — that's our actual
   situation).
2. **Our own account weekly** — views/likes/comments per post, so decisions
   run on our data instead of feelings. 38 followers today is 6 posts of
   noise; by ~post 20 the numbers start meaning something.

## 6. Decision gates

- **Aug 10** (after reels 6–11 have run): read our per-post views.
  - <1k avg → distribution problem: slow to 1/day, investigate AI-label
    damping, lean harder on TikTok/YT.
  - 2k–15k → normal cold start: hold cadence, keep shipping.
  - >15k with flat follows → conversion problem: profile/product work, not
    more reels.
- **First 100 link clicks**: if purchases = 0, fix the product page before
  making another reel.
- **Weekly**: kill the worst-performing format band, add tickets to the best.
