Raw data in this folder: `search_raw.json`, `profiles.json`,
`yangmunus_posts.json` (200 posts), `tiktok_monks.json`, `videos/` (14 top

## The single biggest finding

**This niche is clones of clones, and the scripts are shared property.**
- yangmunus's #1 video (1.25M plays) is monkcheen's BIG01 glass-of-water script
  — near-verbatim, same hook, same "write I will do it below", same close.
- yangmunus's #2 (958k, 14,692 comments) is monkcheen's morning-affirmation
  script (our planned reel 12). Two accounts, same script, 1M+ combined.
- mingsanhealing (TikTok) runs monkcheen's lung-drink and cinnamon-blueberry
  scripts.

Implication: recreating proven scripts IS the niche's standard practice, our
approach is validated, and a script proven on 2 accounts is a stronger bet
than a script proven on 1.

71,000 followers, 55 videos, 446.8k likes. Sells "Jurnalul Pierdut al
Tămăduitorilor Tibetani" (digestive-fire / 21-day weight-loss journal) via
stan.store — checkout link in bio, every caption points to it.
Raw data: `calugarul_wang.json`.

What it proves:
- The monk format transplants to SMALL LANGUAGE MARKETS: 71k followers in
  Romanian, top video 105k plays, on 55 videos. Less competition per capita
  than English.
- Their catalogue is a MONO-TOPIC funnel (every video sells the same
  digestive-fire journal) — narrower than monkcheen but with relentless
  link-pushing in captions.
- stan.store works fine as the checkout for this niche (alternative to
  Shopify/Gumroad).
- Their vein is weight loss — tier-2/3 territory under our truth rule; ours
  would need clean framing if a Romanian mirror is ever built.
