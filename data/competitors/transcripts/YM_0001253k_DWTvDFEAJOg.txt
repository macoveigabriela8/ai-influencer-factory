Place a glass of water beside your bed tonight. Tomorrow morning you will understand why. My friend, this is one of the oldest practices that exist, simple, silent and revealing. Before you sleep, fill a glass with clean water. Hold it with both hands. Look at the water and say in a low voice, while I rest, cleanse what I cannot see. Absorb what is heavy. Remove what is not mine. Water is the most receptive element in nature. It responds to the intention of the one who touches it. Place the glass beside your bed and sleep. In the morning, observe it before anything else. Bubbles, as if it boiled. The energy around you is heavy. The water absorbed it. Cloudy or changed. Weeks, maybe months of tension that was never yours. Clear and still. Your space is light. Energy is flowing. Never drink it. Pour it away. It has done its work. Wash the glass under running water. If it was not clear, repeat for seven nights. Observe the pattern. Do not fear what you find. Fear how long you have been sleeping beside it without knowing. If you will place a glass beside your bed tonight, write, I will do it below. Come back tomorrow and tell me what you found. Not all tiredness comes from the body, my friend. Sometimes you are sleeping beside what weighs you down, and you do not even know it.
