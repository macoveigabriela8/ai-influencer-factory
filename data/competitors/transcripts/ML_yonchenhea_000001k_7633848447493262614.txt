CAPTION Carrying the weight gets easier #healing
---
You may think you have to fight grief to survive it. Push it down. Stay busy, keep going, pretend you are okay. But sometimes the fight is what makes it feel heavier. Grief is not something you defeat. It is something you learn to sit beside. At first, it can feel like a wave that takes the air from your chest. One memory, one song, one empty chair, and suddenly you are back inside the pain. But the pain does not stay the same forever. Even when it feels endless. Even when your mind tells you this is how life will always feel. It will move, it will soften, it will change shape. The love stays. But the sharpness of the grief can settle. Not because you forgot them, and definitely not because you stopped caring. Instead, your heart slowly learns how to carry what happened. So do not shame yourself for drowning today. Just breathe.
