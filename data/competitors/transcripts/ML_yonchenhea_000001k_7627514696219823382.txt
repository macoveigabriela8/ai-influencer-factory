CAPTION The More You Know, the Less You Understand #wisdom #lifelessons #usahealth #healing
---
When you know very little about the world, you feel absolutely certain about everything. But the painful truth is that the more life actually teaches you, the less you realize. You know, you used to think people were simple and that choices were perfectly clear. Yet as you live more, you lose people. You see betrayal, and you watch good people suffer. You learn that right and wrong are rarely easy to separate. All of that youthful certainty starts to aggressively crack. You feel like you are losing your mind because you no longer cling to easy answers. But ancient wisdom teaches that this is not confusion. This is actually profound growth. Wisdom does not make you louder. It makes you quieter. True peace does not come from having life figured out. It comes from finally being honest about exactly what you do not know.
