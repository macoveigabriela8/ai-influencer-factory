The next time you feel excluded, when people make plans and forget your name, let them. When someone enjoys your presence but avoids choosing you fully, let them. When a partner doesn't walk beside you in the small efforts, or your workplace shifts in ways you didn't ask for, let them. So much of our suffering comes from trying to manage other people's choices, moods and timing. We exhaust ourselves fighting currents we were never meant to control. Peace returns when you release your grip and allow people to be exactly who they are, because when you let them, they reveal themselves. And clarity is a gift. When you see who someone truly is, you are no longer confused, no longer guessing, no longer begging for signs. You are free to choose what aligns with you next, so let them go where they choose. And you go where you are honored. Let them. Follow this page if you're done chasing clarity from people who won't give it. More truth like this daily.
