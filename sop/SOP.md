# SOP — Spiritual-Wisdom Reel Factory
### Repeatable system for a faceless AI-persona account selling a digital calm/stillness guide

Derived from teardown of 271 Monk Chen reels and 61 transcripts. Rules trace to `analysis/VIRALITY_ANALYSIS.md`.

**Read this first:** the strongest measured effect in the entire corpus is **posting date** (`r = −0.559`) — cold-start, not craft. Script mechanics barely predict performance *within* his catalogue. So Part 4 (launch strategy) matters more than any rule in Parts 1–2. Treat the script rules as a quality floor, and spend your real attention on the launch window.

### Part 0b — the truth rule (replaces "no medical claims", 2026-08-07)

The old rule banned a **topic**. That was too crude: it threw away
`DVpPFVSDIPj` (1.665% comment rate, the best of any unused video in his
catalogue) because the script says *"the air in your lungs"*. Folk wellness is
most of his catalogue and most of it is harmless and true.

**The test is the claim, not the subject.** Before writing, check every factual
sentence. Three tiers:

| tier | rule | example |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **True + harmless** | Ship it. Keep his hook verbatim. | Soaked chia forms a mucilage gel that slows gastric emptying and increases satiety — real, with human RCT support. |
| ⚠️ **True tip, false mechanism** | Keep the tip, rewrite the mechanism. His explanations are often invented even when the practice is fine. | Chia is genuinely filling — but it does **not** "drag out accumulated fat" and lemon does **not** "kickstart fat burning". Cut those, keep the seeds. |
| 🛑 **False, and harmful if believed** | Do not produce. No rewrite salvages it. | Alkaline food "starving" cancer; a drink that "cleanses" a smoker's lungs. |

**Why the third tier is absolute:** blood pH is held constant by the lungs and
kidneys and cannot be moved by food. The tissue around a tumour is acidic
*because* cancer cells pump acid out — the acidity is a **consequence** of the
disease, not a cause of it. The video teaches the causality backwards, and
someone acting on it may delay real treatment. Our narrator is a synthetic
person claiming forty years of authority; that makes a false health claim worse
coming from us than from him, not better.

**Blocked sources under this rule:** `DZRx8QWBMAM` (cancer), `DVhiZP8jCMU`
(cancer), `DWjoJoMEfoe` + `DWUopecEYcg` (lung cleansing), `DW-PDV4jA7X` as
written (fat loss "as fast as possible" via invented thermogenesis/insulin
mechanisms — its 1.2M plays are not worth it, and IG restricts weight-loss
claims separately).

Verify with a search when unsure. "He said it" is not verification.

### ⚠️ On length — measured across all 266 videos (2026-07-25)

| band | n | median plays | best |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90–120s | 6 | 10,186 | 31,369 |
| 120–150s | 189 | 8,280 | 580,071 |
| 150s+ | 71 | 10,104 | **4,047,769** |

Range: **99s min, 143s median, 170s max. Correlation duration↔plays: r = +0.113.**

Two conclusions, and the second is the important one:

1. Within his range, length barely predicts anything (r = +0.113, weakly *positive* — longer is if anything slightly better). Variance lives in hook and script, not duration.
2. **He has zero videos under 99s, so his data says nothing about short-form.** Do not cite him for or against it. It is an open question.

**Current decision: produce at 30–60s and treat it as a live experiment.** The justification is economic, not performance — Seedance bills a flat $0.205/finished second, so a 45s reel costs ~$9 against ~$31 at his native length. Same budget, ~3× the shots on goal, against a variable that barely correlates. Ship a ~150s control alongside the short ones and let the account's *own* data settle it within two weeks; that data will be better than his, because it will actually contain short videos.

## PART 1 — Topic selection (do this before writing)

### The topic must pass all three gates

**Gate 1 — Is it *identity*, not *information*?**
Information ("cinnamon lowers blood sugar") is a commodity; thousands post it. Identity content implicates the viewer personally.

| ✅ Post these | ❌ Never these |
|---|---|
| Dreams about the dead | Juice/blender recipes |
| Birth dates & destiny | "Pharmacies hate this" |
| First daughter / birth order | Any disease claim |
| Things said to your mother | Weight-loss supplements |
| Objects in your home holding energy | Detox teas |
| Night-time rituals | Anything a doctor should say |

**Gate 2 — Can it be done tonight, for free, with what's already in the house?**
Glass of water. Salt. A spoon. A garlic clove. Your own hands. Cost of entry must be zero — that's what converts a viewer into a *participant*.

**Gate 3 — Does it branch?** *(soft gate — prefer, don't require)*
You should be able to write "if X → it means A; if Y → it means B."

⚠️ **Correction from v1.0 of this SOP:** this was previously stated as "the strongest measured predictor of virality (64% of hits, 0% of flops)" and enforced as a hard gate. **That finding did not replicate** — across 61 transcripts, branching shows `r = −0.066` against plays, and his #3 reel (1.2M) has no branching at all. Keep the device: it structures retention, fills comments, and costs nothing. But do **not** reject a strong identity topic because it will not branch. Gates 1 and 2 are the real filters.

### The seven proven topic veins
1. **Night rituals with a morning reveal** — highest performing vein (his #1)
2. **Dreams as messages** — especially the dead (his #2)
3. **Birth date / birth order as destiny** — instant self-relevance
4. **Family words that leave scars** — mothers, fathers, children
5. **Household objects holding energy** — doors, mirrors, corners, thresholds
6. **The body as a messenger** — tension, sighing, waking at 3am (emotional, *never* medical)
7. **Money/luck blockages** — cleansing rituals for stagnation

## PART 2 — The 7-beat script template

Write to this skeleton every time. Target 380–400 words.

```
[0–8s]    BEAT 1 — HOOK
          Imperative + delayed payoff.
          "Place a glass of water beside your bed tonight.
           Tomorrow morning you will understand why."
          RULES: second person. Concrete object. Time-bound ("tonight").
          Never explain in the hook — promise a reveal.

[8–15s]   BEAT 2 — AUTHORITY FRAME
          "This is one of the oldest cleansing practices that exist."
          Ancient / secret / nearly-lost. 1–2 sentences. Do not linger.

[15–45s]  BEAT 3 — THE INSTRUCTION
          Precise, sensory, numbered or sequenced.
          Include one small strange detail ("hold it with both hands",
          "without speaking to anyone first") — specificity reads as authenticity.
          Include the words to say aloud, if any. Scripts travel.

[45–85s]  BEAT 4 — DIAGNOSTIC BRANCHES  ← THE RETENTION ENGINE
          3–4 branches. "If it is X → it means A."
          Order them: alarming → concerning → reassuring.
          Every viewer must find themselves in exactly one branch.
          This is what forces the watch to the end and fills the comments.

[85–100s] BEAT 5 — WARNING + ESCALATION
          One prohibition ("never drink it") — a rule implies real power.
          Then escalate to a streak: "if not clean after the first night,
          do it seven nights in a row." Turns one view into a week-long habit.

[100–120s] BEAT 6 — THE ABSOLUTION  ← THE EMOTIONAL PAYLOAD
          Reframe the viewer's suffering as external and not their fault.
          "Not all exhaustion comes from the body, my friend.
           Sometimes what weighs you down is invisible."
          This is the beat people screenshot and share.

[120–135s] BEAT 7a — SCRIPTED COMMENT CTA
          Give the EXACT words to type.
          "Write 'I will do it today' in the comments, and come back
           tomorrow to tell me what you found in the glass."
          The return-visit request is worth more than the comment itself.

[135–155s] BEAT 7b — PRODUCT BRIDGE
          Pivot from the tactic to the ROOT CAUSE, then sell freedom
          from needing tactics at all:
          "If you are tired of needing a ritual every time something feels heavy…
           I spent over 20 years learning in silence how to live without them.
           Everything I know is inside <PRODUCT>. The link is in my profile."
```

### The 45s compression (added 2026-07-25)

The skeleton above is 150s-shaped. For the 30–60s experiment, cut **beats 2 and 5** and compress beat 4 — do **not** shave every beat evenly, or you lose the payload and keep the padding.

```
[0-5s]    BEAT 1 — HOOK          verbatim from his reel. Never rewrite the hook.
[5-18s]   BEAT 3 — INSTRUCTION   keep the one strange specific detail.
[18-30s]  BEAT 4 — BRANCHES      2 branches, not 4. Alarming -> reassuring.
[30-40s]  BEAT 6 — ABSOLUTION    <- never cut this. It is the shareable beat.
[40-45s]  BEAT 7  — CTA          comment CTA on screen + link CTA spoken.
```

Cut beat 2 (authority frame) because a 45s viewer has not invested enough to need credentials, and beat 5 (warning/escalation) because a streak request needs earned trust. Beat 6 survives at any length — it is what people screenshot.

≈115–130 words at 145–160 wpm.

### Line-level rules
- **Second person throughout.** "You", never "one" or "people".
- **Short sentences.** Average 8–12 words. One idea per subtitle card.
- **No hedging.** Never "may", "might", "some believe". Declarative only.
- **One term of endearment per script** — "my friend". Used sparingly, it lands.
- **Never explain the mechanism scientifically.** "Water is the most receptive element in nature" is the right register. Chemistry is the wrong register.

## PART 3 — Production pipeline

**Rewritten 2026-07-25 from a full production run (Reel B, ~$80 of generations).** The previous version of this section recommended an audio-driven avatar (OmniHuman / InfiniteTalk / Hedra) producing one unbroken 150s take. **That architecture did not survive contact with the tools.** What follows is what actually works, with the measurements that prove it. Do not re-litigate these — they cost real money to establish.

### The architecture

**Seedance 2.0 on kie, driven by `reference_audio_urls` + `reference_image_urls`.**

One generation produces, together: our script spoken, lip-sync exact by construction, the voice identity from our ElevenLabs reference, clean hands, and no skin-texture decay. It replaces the entire "animate then lip-sync" pipeline.

#### Why not the old plan

| Approach | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| kie `volcengine/video-to-video-lip-sync` | **Dead. Do not attempt.** | Well-formed tasks sit in `state: "waiting"` indefinitely at `creditsConsumed: 0.0`, `failMsg: null`. Confirmed via `/jobs/recordInfo`. There is no post-hoc lip-sync available to us. |
| OmniHuman avatar | **Rejected — skin decay.** | Forearm-patch Laplacian variance falls 9.0 → 4.1 (−55%) across 15s. This is the "young plastic arms" defect. Seedance holds 386 → 370 (−4%). |
| One unbroken 150s take | **Not achievable.** | Seedance caps at 15s per generation, and its reference-audio field caps at 15s too. A 150s reel is necessarily ~11 generations. |

**Accept the cuts.** His reels have none; ours cut every ~14s. Empirically the joins read as ordinary cuts rather than as errors, because every clip is anchored to the same reference image. This is a real, known deviation from his format — track whether it costs us, don't pretend it isn't there.

### Hard constraints (each learned by hitting it)

1. **`reference_audio_urls` is mutually exclusive with `first_frame_url`/`last_frame_url`.** HTTP 422: *"The reference audio and the first and last frames are mutually exclusive, and only one scene can be selected."* Use `reference_image_urls` when audio is present. Continuity still holds.

2. **Reference audio is a SCRIPT DRIVER, not a voice sample.** The output speaks our words but *re-performs them at its own pace* — waveform correlation against our chunk is only 0.148 at −660ms offset. Consequences:
   - **You cannot mux the original ElevenLabs mp3 back over the video.** It will desync. Keep Seedance's own audio.
   - Voice consistency comes from every chunk being a slice of **one continuous ElevenLabs recording**.
   - **Caption timing must come from the rendered audio, never the script.** Whisper word-timestamps on the actual output is the only source of truth.

3. **⚠️ THE LOUDNESS THRESHOLD — the single most valuable finding here.** Reference chunks below **~0.048 RMS** come back as generic spiritual filler instead of our script. Above ~0.050 they come back verbatim. Measured across 8 generations; clip 8 failed twice at 0.0473 RMS, then rendered verbatim after normalising to 0.1515.

   **Always** run `loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11` on every chunk. It is free and changes level, not delivery.

   This matters because the quiet passages are the *tender* ones — the absolution and the CTA, i.e. exactly the beats the reel cannot afford to lose.

4. **Billing is flat per second: 41 credits/s = $0.205/s** (1 credit = $0.005). 15s=615cr, 14s=574cr, 12s=492cr, 5s=205cr. There is **no** packing penalty — an earlier "19% packing tax" claim was wrong. The only overhead is rounding up to whole seconds (~3%).

5. **Yield is ~75%.** Budget ~25–30% extra for re-rolls. Always transcribe every clip before assembling.

6. **Chunks under ~8s are unreliable**, and a chunk asking for a social-media action may never render at all — see the known-unsolved note below.

### The steps

```
STEP 1 — CHARACTER ANCHOR  (once, ~$0.02)
  One frontal still, chest-up, hands resting, eye-line to lens.
  Saved to output/assets/kaion_rest.url — EVERY clip anchors to it.
  Without it there is no identity continuity across generations.

STEP 2 — VOICE  (~$0.20 per script)
  ElevenLabs, calm elderly male, 145-160 wpm. ONE continuous recording
  for the whole script -> output/assets/voice_B.mp3
  Do not record per-chunk: identity drifts between takes.

STEP 3 — CHUNK PLAN  ->  output/assets/chunks.json
  List of {n, s, e, dur, text}. Cut on SENTENCE boundaries, 12-15s each.
  Never open or close a chunk mid-word.
  Video seconds = int(dur) + 1, capped at 15 -- round UP, so the clip
  never ends before the sentence does.

STEP 4 — GENERATE       python3 scripts/render_reel.py 2 3 4
  Normalises (loudnorm), uploads, submits. ~3-7 min per clip.

STEP 5 — VERIFY EVERY CLIP  <- NON-NEGOTIABLE GATE
  (a) TEXT. Transcribe each output and diff against the intended text.
      A failed generation is fluent and confident, not obviously broken --
      it returns polished generic wisdom in the right voice. It will ship
      silently if you do not check.
  (b) MUSIC. Seedance beds SOME generations with a drone (confirmed on
      seedance_08: A2/A3/D4 in its quiet frames). Detect by SPECTRUM, not
      by level -- FFT the quietest ~25% of frames and look for sharp
      partials on note frequencies. A noise-floor test does NOT catch it:
      the bedded clip measured 0.95% of peak, indistinguishable from room
      tone on amplitude alone. Harmonic stack = re-roll.

STEP 6 — ASSEMBLE      python3 scripts/assemble_reel.py 1 2 3 4 5 8 7
  Concat -> Whisper -> caption cards -> 1080x1920 master.
  Add --music only for the fallback build (see Step 7).
```

### Captions — the rules, all learned the hard way

- **This ffmpeg build has NO libass, libfreetype or drawtext.** Verified via `ffmpeg -filters`. Text *cannot* be burned from inside a filtergraph. Cards are drawn as RGBA PNGs by PIL and composited via the concat demuxer as a single alpha track — one overlay regardless of card count.
- **Hold each card until the next one starts.** Showing only its own word span leaves the frame bare in every inter-word gap — ~34 strobes/minute.
- **Instagram safe zones on 1080×1920:** bottom ~430px (caption/username/audio), right ~190px (action rail), top ~230px. Text outside these is text nobody reads.
- **Caption band at 0.72H.** At 0.55H it sits on the speaker's chin.
- **Wrap by measured pixel width, not character count.** "Will" and "mmmm" are four characters and very different widths.
- **Hard-cap cards at 2 rows** by measuring and splitting at a word boundary. A single 3-row card makes the whole block jump vertically mid-reel. Word count only approximates height.

### Step 4 — Music  ⚠️ REVISED

**Do not synthesise the bed.** The previous SOP's recipe (sustained G3+B♭3 sine partials) is a **continuous tone** — no attack, no decay, no note changes — and is physically fatiguing to listen to. Sustained pure sines give the ear no transient to latch onto and park their energy in fixed narrow bands. Rejected on listening, twice; raising the level made it worse.

What his bed actually is, measured off BIG01's quietest 12% (18.8s of music-only material): dominant partials at **233Hz (B♭3)** and **207Hz (A♭3)** — a sustained low pad a major second apart, deliberately ambiguous between major and minor. It does not tell the viewer how to feel; the words do that.

**Preferred: add the track inside Instagram.** Licensed, real recordings, and it places the reel on that track's audio page — a second discovery surface an uploaded bed forfeits. ⚠️ Caveat: the licensed catalogue is materially smaller for **business/creator accounts**, so check it when the account is set up. (This tension with "original audio only" in Part 0 is unresolved — his top 40 are all original audio, but he is not competing with our cut-every-14s problem.)

**Fallback: a real royalty-free track**, mixed with the existing duck. Mood — **melancholic ambient, reflective, not sad**: inspirational major-key fights a grief script, and fully sad does the emotional work the understatement is supposed to do. Search: ambient piano, 60–70 BPM, sparse, minor/modal, no drums, no build.

If a bed is used, `MUSIC_RATIO` in `assemble_reel.py` is the one number to tune. Note RMS-matching a synthetic bed to a real piano under-delivers badly — equal RMS is nowhere near equal loudness.

### ⚠️ KNOWN UNSOLVED — the spoken comment CTA

**The comment-CTA line has never rendered, across four generations.** Failures ranged from word salad ("have to round a name anything to just the name is") to a fluent unrelated platitude ("The path to peace lies not in changing the world…"). Ruled out: loudness (normalised), duration (tried at 5.7s and 11.4s), and CTA-content-alone (clip 8 has no CTA and failed until normalised, then rendered fine).

Best current read: **the monk character prior resists speaking a social-media instruction.** Do not burn more credits re-rolling it blind.

**Workaround in production:** the CTA is drawn on screen instead — a dark band at the top from 0:15 onward (so a mid-scroll arrival sees the ask), plus a closing card. The close shows *different* words top and bottom ("Write their name in the comments" / "Just the name. Nothing else.") — the same text twice reads as a rendering fault, and naming the exact scope is what removes the hesitation that stops people commenting.

This preserves the mechanic that earns his 10,942 comments. **His structure is comment-CTA first, link-CTA last** — both, in that order, confirmed in BIG01 and BIG03 transcripts.

## PART 4 — Launch strategy (cold-start is non-renewable)

The data is unambiguous: his median reach fell 87% over five months, and his two biggest hits came in his first week. **A new account gets one cold-start window. Spend it on your best material.**

1. **Bank 15 finished reels before posting a single one.** Score them all. Post in descending score order.
2. **Post 1–2 per day** (he posts ~1.8/day — 271 posts in 145 days).
3. **First 10 posts must all score ≥90.** No warm-ups, no experiments. The algorithm is deciding what your account *is* during this window.
4. **Reply to every comment for the first 30 days** — especially the branch-reports ("mine was cloudy!"). Comment replies compound the signal that made the reel work.
5. **Bio + link live from post #1.** Traffic is worthless if there's nowhere to send it.
6. **Watch for fatigue.** When median plays drop 3 weeks running, rotate the topic vein and the backdrop set before the decay compounds.
